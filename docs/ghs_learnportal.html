GHS 2026 National IPC Protocols & Cholera Management Guidelines

5 CPD Credits
Mandatory Protocol Compliance Notice

Issued by the Director-General, Ghana Health Service. Applies to all regional teaching hospitals, district facilities, and CHPS compounds.

1. Standard Hand Hygiene & Barrier Precautions

All clinical personnel must adhere strictly to the 5 Moments for Hand Hygiene using alcohol-based hand rub or WHO-formulated soap formulations. In high-density outpatient departments (OPD), PPE usage must align with hazard level tiers.

2. Isolation & Waterborne Outbreak Triage Workflow

When suspected cases are flagged at reception, isolate immediately in designated Holding Bay B. Ensure rapid diagnostic test (RDT) kits are drawn within 15 minutes of intake.

Clinical Case Definition Threshold:
  • Acute watery diarrhea in patients aged 5 years or older.
  • Severe dehydration with rapid onset in endemic districts.
  • Required notification time to District Health Directorate: < 2 Hours.
