Maternal & Neonatal Health • Space: EmONC Protocol 2026

GHS National Guidelines on Postpartum Hemorrhage (PPH) Management

5 NMC CPD Credits
GHS Mandatory First-Line Treatment Directive

Approved by the Institutional Care Division (ICD) Ghana Health Service. Effective January 2026 across all Regional & District Hospitals.

1. Active Management of the Third Stage of Labor (AMTSL)

Administer 10 IU of Oxytocin IM immediately following the delivery of the anterior shoulder or full body of the infant. If Oxytocin is unavailable due to cold-chain disruption, administer Misoprostol 600 mcg sublingually.

2. First-Line Resuscitation Protocol for Refractory PPH

Initiate bimanual uterine compression and rapid IV crystalloid infusion (Ringer's Lactate or Normal Saline) using two large-bore 16G cannulae while notifying the Specialist Obstetrician on call.
